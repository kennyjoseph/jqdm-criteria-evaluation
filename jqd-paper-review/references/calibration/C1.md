**C1 · Score 3.** Usage numbers come with comparison points: the outlet's share of traffic is set beside that of a similar outlet and of a search engine. One share lacks a comparison point. *Justification:* Citable numbers with benchmarks; one small gap.

**C1 · Score 2.** Each research question gets a citable number, such as a concentration share or a set of correlations. None has a comparison point, such as a randomized network or the same quantity on another platform. *Justification:* The question is answered with numbers, but readers cannot judge how large they are.

**C1 · Score 2.** Shares are reported side by side for three sources, which gives built-in comparisons. One headline claim, that one group 'mostly' used the platform for promotion, has no measured quantity behind it. *Justification:* Citable numbers overall; one headline claim is not quantified.

**C1 · Score 1.** Results are described in words ('many', 'most', 'a growing share'), with numbers only in the figures, and the conclusion restates no number. *Justification:* Readers cannot cite what the paper found.
